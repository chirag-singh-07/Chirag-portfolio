FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Do you work with startups?

Yes, I love working with startups! I understand the need for speed and scalability, and I can help you build an MVP or a full-fledged product.

What makes your web design unique?

I focus on "immersive" experiences. I don't just build pages; I build interactions using modern animations, glassmorphism, and dynamic layouts that keep users engaged.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Absolutely. I can audit your current site, identify pain points, and give it a complete visual and functional overhaul to meet modern standards.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. A landing page might take 3-5 days, while a full commercial platform could take 2-4 weeks. I always provide a clear timeline upfront.
